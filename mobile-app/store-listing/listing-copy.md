## Full description

Works for both stores. Apple's limit is 4,000 characters, Google's is 4,000 —
this is roughly 1,100, which is deliberate; long listings do not convert
better.

```
Tell us what needs doing.

Emmerdale Agriculture is a managed marketplace for rural land. Describe the
job in your own words and we pass it to contractors who cover your area — you
get one price, one booking, and one place to come back to.

Paddock maintenance, topping, harrowing, rolling, overseeding, hedge cutting,
fencing and land clearance, for paddock owners, equestrian yards, farms and
estates across England, Wales and Scotland.

WHY PEOPLE USE US
• Prices upfront — you see the price before you commit
• Pay online, no chasing invoices
• Vetted and fully trained operators
• Fully insured
• Free to ask, with no obligation

HOW IT WORKS
1. Tell us what needs doing, in your own words.
2. We match it against our network of approved contractors who work in your
   county.
3. You get a price. If it works for you, book it online.
4. The operator does the work. You track the job in the app.

We started as Hampshire Paddock Management, looking after paddocks,
smallholdings and grassland across the South of England. The enquiries never
stopped coming, and one firm can only be in so many fields at once — so we
grew into Emmerdale Agriculture: the same standard of work, delivered by
approved operators we know and trust.

FOR CONTRACTORS
Run an agricultural contracting business? Apply to join the network. We bring
you the customer and handle the payment; you set your own price and do the
work you are good at. No fee — you receive 100% of what you quoted.

Emmerdale Agriculture Online Ltd, the company behind Hampshire Paddock
Management.
```

## Keywords (Apple only, 100 char max, comma-separated, no spaces)

```
paddock,topping,harrowing,hedge,fencing,grassland,equestrian,tractor,contractor,farm,smallholding
```
— 97 characters. No need to repeat words already in the app name or subtitle;
Apple indexes those separately, so "agriculture", "land" and "jobs" are
deliberately left out to avoid wasting the budget.

Google Play has no keyword field — it reads the descriptions instead, which is
why the phrases above appear naturally in the full description.
